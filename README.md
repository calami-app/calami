## Why Calami?

Most Markdown apps focus on writing. Calami is built for **both reading and writing** — a single app where you can draft articles, study documents, annotate PDFs, and present slides, all without leaving your workspace.

It's lightweight, cross-platform, and your files never leave your machine.

## Core Features

### 📖 Immersive Reading

Turn any Markdown file into a clean, book-like reading experience. Select a word to get an instant translation, highlight passages with color-coded annotations, or let the app read aloud to you.

<p align="center">
<img src="./assets/reading.png" width="720" alt="Immersive Reading" />
</p>

### ✍️ Writing with AI

Switch between rich-text and source mode anytime. An AI assistant lives in the sidebar — ask it to summarize, continue writing, polish your prose, or translate. Bring your own API key (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or local models all work).

<p align="center">
<img src="./assets/writing.png" width="720" alt="Writing with AI" />
</p>

### 🎬 Presenting

Turn any Markdown file into a slideshow with one click. Present your ideas directly from your notes — no need to switch to PowerPoint or Google Slides.

<p align="center">
<img src="./assets/presenting.png" width="720" alt="Presenting" />
</p>

### 🎨 Make It Yours

Choose from built-in themes and skins, or describe a style in plain language and let AI generate a custom skin for you. Adjust fonts, spacing, and layout to match your taste.

<p align="center">
<img src="./assets/theming.png" width="720" alt="Theming" />

### 📤 Export Anywhere

One click to export as PDF, HTML, Word, PNG, EPUB, or mind map. Publish directly to image hosting services.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>How is Calami different from Typora / Obsidian / Bear?</strong></summary>

Calami treats reading as a first-class experience — immersive mode, instant translation, vocabulary book, bionic reading, and text-to-speech are all built in.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work offline?</strong></summary>

Yes. Everything except cloud-based AI works offline. If you use a local model like Ollama, even AI works without internet.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is my data private?</strong></summary>

Your files live on your machine as standard Markdown. No cloud sync, no account required. AI requests go directly to your chosen provider — Calami never sees your content.

</details>
